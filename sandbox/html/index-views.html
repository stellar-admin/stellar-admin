Index layouts

A layout switcher on Voyager Travel's bookings index. Some layouts draw the same rows differently; others have a data shape of their own, built from the same bookings:

  • Table, Cards, Board: one item per booking.
  • Map: one item per destination (bookings, travellers, revenue, next departure). Its search finds destinations.
  • Calendar: one cell per day, holding that day's departures, a month at a time.
  • Chart: one bar per departure month, revenue stacked by status. No search.

Scopes and filters narrow the bookings every layout is built from, so they carry across. The search belongs to the layout. Every frame is live: scopes, search, filter pills (+ Filter puts them back), the switcher, pins and calendar months. Under each frame are its URL and the shape of what the layout lists.

1. Above the card, right-aligned

The switcher on its own row just outside the card, with and without widgets. Use Switcher in the bar at the bottom right for icons only or icons with labels.

2. Layouts with their own data shape

Each frame opens on one of them; the switcher still works.

3. Screen sizes

Top and aside widgets at laptop, tablet and phone widths, then a phone without widgets.
