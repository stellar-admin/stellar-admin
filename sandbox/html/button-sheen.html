Button sheen

The --sa-sheen knob (on by default), after Bootstrap 6's styled buttons: solid buttons get a gradient over their colour (white at the top, black at the bottom), a 1px highlight along the top edge, a darker edge and a pressed shadow. It sets only the surface; --sa-relief still sets the shadow around the button, so each row pairs one relief with sheen off and on. Hover and press the buttons to see those states.
