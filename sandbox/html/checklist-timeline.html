Checklist and timeline

Two components after steps.html showed one component's switches falling into two fixed sets. sa-checklist is tasks to act on: a rule between items, tinted status icons, actions and details, no rail. sa-timeline is a sequence to read: a rail between markers, dates, and markers that show a status, a number, a dot, an icon or an avatar. Both share the item parts (title, description, actions, details) and the statuses: done, current, to do, optional, warning and error.

Timeline

The tracker is a timeline whose markers show a status. The rail out of an item takes its style from the item's status; an item's rail overrides it.
