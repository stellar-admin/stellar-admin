Popover and combobox multiselects

Hand-built patterns where the choices open next to the field rather than in a sheet. Lists are the real sel-command component with data-filter="none" (↑/↓, Home/End, Enter); multiselect toggles data-checked on the item and keeps the popover open. Popovers are native popover elements with sa-popover-content and CSS anchor positioning. Remote searches go through a fake server with 350 ms latency. Functional enough to feel; not production code.
