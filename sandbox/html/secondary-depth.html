Secondary button depth

Options for the secondary button with sheen on. In dark mode the grey fill is lighter than the surface, so the gradient reads as light on a raised button; in light mode the fill is darker than the surface, so the white highlight barely shows and the gradient mostly darkens. D is what ships now; A is what shipped before. B to F change light mode only; G and H change the colour. Each option shows the page (canvas) and a card (surface). Hover and press the buttons.
