Sheet lookup, multiple items

The existing sheet lookup (sel-command in the shared dashboard sheet, server-paged results, Load more) extended to pick several items. Two independent questions: how the field shows the selection in the form, and how the sheet manages it. The live form below lets you combine any field display with any sheet behaviour; the sections after it lay every variant out side by side. Other pages: index.

Live: in a form

A real sa-sheet dialog opens from the field (standing in for the htmx LookupSheet load). Enter or click toggles the active row; ↑/↓ from the search box. The readout shows what the form would post (repeated name=value pairs).
