Row details emphasis

How an expanded index row and its details are set apart from each other and from the rows around them. Each grid is the AdminPlayground Products index with three rows expanded; the toggles and tabs work. The proposal keeps None, Band and Rail, moves Band to the lighter fill, and adds an optional divider between a row and its details; the control bar switches the divider between inset (starting where the content starts) and full width. The tab alignment fix applies throughout.
