One component or two? Steps, checklists, timelines

Every example on this page uses the same markup: sa-steps with sa-step rows, each holding a marker, a header (title and meta), a description, actions and details. What changes between a checklist, a tracker, numbered instructions, an itinerary and an activity feed is a handful of attributes on the list, the marker type and the statuses used. What the prototype shows

    
Shared: the anatomy (marker, title, meta, description, actions, details) and the statuses (done, current, to do, optional, warning, error). The checklist is a vertical list of steps with no rail and a rule between rows.

    
Out of scope: a horizontal stepper. A booking wizard is form navigation (locked or clickable steps, panels, back and next) and gets its own component later.

    
Where it strains: an activity feed has no status at all; it borrows the marker and rail and nothing else.

    
Precedent: Ant Design, Mantine and Chakra each ship Steps (progress through a process, with a current step) and Timeline (dated events) as separate components with near-identical markup.
