Multi-select lookup

Narrowing down the sheet lookup for collections. The field, the sheet, the result rows and the loading and empty states use the markup the shipped single-value lookup renders, captured from the playground. The exploration this came from is on the sheet and popover pages.

Decided

  • Immediate: each toggle updates the field behind the sheet. There is no draft and no Cancel.
  • One change event when the sheet closes, and only if the value changed. Removing from the field fires it straight away.
  • Enter toggles the active row and selects the search text. Esc, Done, Ctrl or ⌘+Enter and the backdrop all close and keep the selection.
  • The form posts the field's name once per value.
  • Create (6): New in the no-results state and in the sheet footer (B1 + B3), with Alt+N (⌥N on a Mac) from anywhere in the sheet.
  • Footer (7): two rows, the count, Clear and key hints above New and Done.

Proposed, always on: seeing the whole selection. An All | Selected control sits under the search, with the count on Selected. The Selected view searches within the selection, drops a row when it is unchecked, and has Clear all, so the footer no longer holds the count or Clear. A read-only Summary shows the List display.
