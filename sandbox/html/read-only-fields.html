Read-only fields: the platform's behaviour on every editor

Read-only looks like the normal control at rest, at full contrast, and loses only the affordances for changing the value, as Chromium drops a read-only date input's picker icon. You can't change it, it still takes focus and its value is posted. Choice controls lose their hover, since nothing on them responds; controls whose text you can select keep it.

Disabled is faded, takes no focus and isn't posted.

Each row's accent-coloured notes say what changed from today's components (the playground's Editor states page). Each cell is its own form: the line under it shows what it would post and its tab stops, and updates as you interact.
