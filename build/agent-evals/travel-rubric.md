# Rubric

Score each item from 1 to 5 (1 poor, 3 adequate, 5 excellent) with a sentence explaining the score. Base it on what you saw while doing the checklist and on these screenshots, saved in this directory:

- `list.png`: the bookings list, first page.
- `no-results.png`: the bookings list after searching for `zzz`.
- `form.png`: the edit form of trip VT-IT-04 after saving it with the title empty.
- `list-narrow.png`: the bookings list at 390 by 844 pixels.
- `form-narrow.png`: the booking form at 390 by 844 pixels.

Items:

- R1 Visual quality: consistent spacing, alignment, typography and colour; looks like a finished product rather than a scaffold.
- R2 List usability: clear controls for search, filtering, sorting and paging; the current state is visible; an empty result says so.
- R3 Form usability: clear labels, fitting inputs (date pickers for dates, choices for statuses and other fixed values), validation messages next to the fields.
- R4 Narrow screens: the lists and forms are usable at phone width, without content cut off or overlapping.
- R5 Navigation: moving between the kinds of record, their lists and their forms is obvious, and the user always knows where they are.
